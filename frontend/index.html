How it works

User submits a URL
↓
Normalize & de-fang (hxxp→http, [.]→. , collapse slashes)
↓
Allowlist — 245 trusted roots → instant SAFE
↓
Feature extraction — 22 signals + char/word TF-IDF
↓
XGBoost model scores the URL (the decider)
↓
Rule engine — structural overrides + decisive blocklist + weighted signals
↓
Risk score + explanation
↓
Logged to dashboard & Power BI
