How URL detection works

1. Feature extraction
The URL is broken into 22 signals — length, subdomain depth, brand look-alikes, raw IP, @-tricks, suspicious TLDs, punycode/IDN homographs and more — plus character- and word-level TF-IDF.
2. Trusted-root guard
Known-legitimate domains (exact registrable-root match) are cleared instantly, so real sites are never wrongly flagged.
3. ML + rules
An XGBoost model scores the URL; structural rules override it for unambiguous attacks like @-redirects and raw-IP hosts.
4. Risk score
You get a 0–10 risk score, a clear verdict, and the exact reasons behind it.
